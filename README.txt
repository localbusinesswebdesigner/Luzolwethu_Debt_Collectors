LUZOLWETHU DEBT COLLECTORS WEBSITE

FILES
- index.html: website content and page structure
- styles.css: colours, layout and mobile responsiveness
- script.js: mobile navigation and enquiry form-to-WhatsApp behaviour
- assets/images/: place your own image files here

ADD YOUR LOCAL PICTURES
Copy your image files into assets/images and rename them to these exact names:
1. hero.jpg       - wide banner image for the top of the website
2. about-team.jpg - office, team, or professional business photo
3. logo.png       - your company logo (transparent PNG works best)
4. favicon.png    - small square icon (optional)

If you use different filenames, edit the matching paths in index.html and styles.css.
The site is designed to use local images and does not need an image service. Google Fonts are loaded online; if you want the site fully offline, remove the @import line at the top of styles.css and the site will use fallback fonts.

PREVIEW
Open index.html in a web browser. For publishing, upload index.html, styles.css, script.js and the entire assets folder to your hosting provider's website root.

BEFORE PUBLISHING
- Confirm that the business name, WhatsApp number (+27 65 978 1855), service area and all service descriptions are correct.
- Replace the sample photos/logo with your own images.
- Add a real privacy notice and any required legal/business registration details after checking the requirements that apply to your business. Do not claim authorisation or registration unless confirmed.
- The enquiry form opens WhatsApp with a prefilled message; it does not store or email form submissions.
- Replace the canonical URL if your final domain is different.
