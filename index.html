IMPORTANT NOTICE TO DEBTORS

WARNING: FAILURE TO PAY YOUR DEBT MAY HAVE SERIOUS CONSEQUENCES

Have you failed to pay your outstanding account? Ignoring your debt will not make it disappear. Failure to settle your account or make acceptable payment arrangements may result in further recovery action.

Legal Action

Continued non-payment may result in legal proceedings to recover the outstanding amount, subject to applicable laws and procedures.

Negative Credit Bureau Listing

Failure to meet your payment obligations may result in adverse credit information being reported to a credit bureau where legally permitted and after the required procedures have been followed. This may affect your ability to obtain credit in the future.

Additional Recovery Costs

Where permitted by law or the applicable agreement, further recovery or legal costs may be incurred.

DO NOT IGNORE YOUR OUTSTANDING ACCOUNT.

Take action now. Contact Luzolwethu Debt Collectors to discuss your outstanding balance and available payment arrangements.

Clear Information. Appropriate Next Steps.

Outstanding debt can have financial consequences, but the outcome depends on the circumstances, the applicable law and the steps properly taken by the relevant parties.

Credit information may be reported only where legally permitted and applicable requirements are met. Court action or a judgment is not automatic simply because an account is unpaid.

If you receive a collection communication, check the identity of the creditor, the amount claimed and the supporting information. Raise any dispute promptly through the appropriate channel.

Frequently asked questions

Questions? We've Got Answers.

Here are a few general answers to common questions about account recovery.

Ask us a question →
What information should I provide when making an enquiry?

Start with your business contact details, the type of support you need and a brief overview. Avoid sending identity documents or sensitive financial information until a secure and appropriate channel has been agreed.

Can you guarantee that an outstanding debt will be recovered?

No. Recovery depends on factors such as the validity of the debt, available information, the debtor's circumstances and applicable legal requirements. No specific outcome can be guaranteed.

Does unpaid debt automatically lead to blacklisting or a court judgment?

No. Credit reporting and legal proceedings are subject to applicable laws and requirements. An unpaid account does not automatically mean a court judgment has been granted.

Can a payment arrangement be discussed?

Where appropriate and agreed by the relevant parties, payment arrangements may be discussed based on the account and the circumstances.

How do I contact Luzolwethu Debt Collectors?

Use the contact form below or contact us on WhatsApp. Please do not include passwords, full banking credentials or unnecessary sensitive personal information in your first enquiry.
